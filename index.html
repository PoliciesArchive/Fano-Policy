Host policy

Gift Share

If a host receives 900K coins, the host receives 900K diamonds in their wallet.

Host Share

The host automatically receives 200% of the Gift Share.

Example: if a host receives 900K diamonds, they receive an extra 1.8M diamonds as Host Share.

Room Bonus

The host receives 30% of the room receiving.

How your earnings are calculated

Start with the diamonds you complete toward your ID target. Host Share and Room Bonus are added on top, then the total is converted to USD at the withdrawal rate.

  • Gift Share equals your diamonds × 100%.
  • Host Share equals your Gift Share × 200%.
  • Room Bonus equals your diamonds × 30%.
  • Total equals all three added together, divided by 1,500,000 for USD.
